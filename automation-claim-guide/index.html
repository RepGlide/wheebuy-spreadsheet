Automation Claim Guide

Read automated, smart, live, sync, and viral with the evidence boundary each one needs.

A word is not a capability

An automation word in a title is a label, not a confirmed ability. It becomes a capability only when it explains what is automated, how, and for whom.

automated

What is automated, exactly? A page that says “automated” without saying what is automatic has not shown an automation.

smart

Smart by what measure, and on what data? A “smart” with no measure is a preference, not a feature.

live

Live from what source, updated when? A word is not a feed. Ask where the live data comes from.

sync

Sync between what and what? Without the two ends, the word means little.

viral

Viral where, and measured how? Popularity is a description, not a capability you can rely on.

?Open item: is any automation word being read as a confirmed capability?

A worked read

  1. Word: a title says “smart sync”.
  2. Evidence: no source, no pair, no measure is shown.
  3. Result: the words stay labels, not abilities.
