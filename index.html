What a Spreadsheet Can and Cannot Do

Organize, Yes. Confirm, No.

1A spreadsheet can organize

  • Public entries and category groupings.
  • Links to open later.
  • A place to hold your own notes.

2A spreadsheet cannot confirm

  • The source page a link now opens.
  • The exact option or current value.
  • The final destination a jump reaches.

The Boundary Switchboard

Four Static Zones

The knobs are decoration. The four zones are the real boundary between organizing and confirming.

Organize

Lay out public entries and categories. This is what the sheet does well.

Observe

Read the page and note what it visibly shows right now.

Recheck

Confirm the option, value, and destination on the actual page.

Stop

When a link jumps, changes, or goes missing — stop before reusing the old note.

Automation Language Needs Evidence

A Word Is Not a Capability

automated, smart, live, sync, and viral are labels until they explain what, how, and for whom.

  • 1

    automated. What is automated, exactly? The label does not say.

  • 2

    smart. Smart by what measure, and on what data? Ask.

  • 3

    live. Live from what source, updated when? A word is not a feed.

  • 4

    sync. Sync between what and what? Without the pair, it means little.

  • 5

    viral. Viral where, and measured how? Popularity is not a capability.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a WheeBuy spreadsheet?

A WheeBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It organizes public entries; it does not confirm them.

Is this website the official WheeBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by WheeBuy or Google.

Does the manual checkpoint automate or track anything?

No. The manual checkpoint is a rechecking routine. It is not an automation platform, dropshipping system, product database, store, QC certification, order tracker, or fee calculator.

What does an automation word actually prove?

Nothing by itself. Words like automated, smart, live, sync, and viral are labels, not confirmed capabilities. They need evidence about what is automated, how, and for whom.

What four things should I manually recheck?

The source page, the exact option, the current value (currency, quantity, time, and conditions), and the final destination the link actually reaches.

Where do I place an order?

After you have manually rechecked the four checkpoints, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
